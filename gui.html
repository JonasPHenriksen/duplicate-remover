Photo cleanup

Moves duplicate photos and videos, and files that aren't photos or videos, into a _Quarantine folder. Nothing is deleted unless you empty it, and everything can be put back until then.

1. Choose a folder

Options
What to look for

Documents, sidecar files and the like move to _Quarantine/not-media. Untick to look only for duplicates.

JPEGs whose pictures are identical, but whose tags differ, such as dates, ratings or captions a photo app wrote, count as duplicates too. The copy with the most tags is preferred. Copies turned another way, or with other colour profiles, don't count.

Also lists copies at another size or quality, and shots taken a moment apart. Slower, as every photo is opened, and only for JPEG, PNG and GIF. Nothing in the list moves unless you tick it.

Which copy stays

When nothing else decides, the copy with the latest date stays, rather than the earliest.

Folders whose copies always stay

Copies of photos and videos in these folders, inside the one you clean, never move, and stay rather than their copies elsewhere.

    Copies in folders with these names move before others, as they do in Backup, DCIM, Camera Roll, Downloads, WhatsApp Images and the like. Separate the names with commas.

    Reference folder

    Photos and videos already in this folder, outside the one you clean, count as kept: all their copies in the folder you clean move. Nothing in the reference folder changes.

    Where to look
    Folders to leave out

    These folders, inside the one you clean, are never looked in, so nothing in them moves.

      After moving

      Folders the move empties are removed. Undo makes them again.

      Speed

      Faster on most disks. Untick it for spinning hard disks, which read one file at a time faster. On Linux they are noticed by themselves.

      Keeps what it learns about each file in _Quarantine, so that the next look through this folder only reads the files that are new or changed.
